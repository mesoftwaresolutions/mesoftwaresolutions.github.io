ARIVU

Automated Reach, Insights & Visibility Utility

ARIVU is an internal content publishing tool. We use it to run our own social media pages (for example GenZ Kural, short Tamil life lessons).

What it does

  • Turns rows from our own Google Sheet into images and short videos.
  • Uploads private previews to our own Google Drive so we can review them.
  • After we approve a post, publishes it to our own Instagram, Facebook Page, Threads and YouTube accounts.
  • Collects public performance numbers (views, likes, followers) into our own Google Sheet dashboard.

ARIVU only acts on accounts that we own and have connected ourselves. It is not a public service and does not collect data from other people.
