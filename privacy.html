Privacy Policy

ARIVU · Last updated: 2 November 2026

ARIVU is an internal tool operated by ME Software Solutions to publish content on social media accounts that we own. This policy explains what data ARIVU accesses and how it is handled.

Who uses ARIVU

Only ME Software Solutions uses ARIVU, with our own Google, Meta (Instagram, Facebook, Threads) and YouTube accounts. ARIVU is not offered to the public and does not collect personal data from visitors or other users.

Google data

  • Google Drive: ARIVU uses the drive.file permission. It can only see and change files that ARIVU itself created (preview images and videos in one preview folder). It cannot read any other files in our Drive.
  • Google Sheets: ARIVU reads and updates our own content sheet (post text, status, links).
  • YouTube: ARIVU uploads videos we approved to our own channel and reads that channel's public statistics.

ARIVU's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

ARIVU Dashboard

The ARIVU Dashboard shows a page's upcoming posts and statistics. It runs only in your browser; we run no server for it. After you sign in with Google it uses:

  • Google Sheets (read-only, spreadsheets.readonly): reads the content sheet and analytics sheet that the signed-in account can open, to show the queue and statistics. It cannot change any sheet.
  • Google Drive app data (drive.appdata): saves only the dashboard's own settings file (sheet links, publish time, goals) in the account's hidden app folder. It cannot see any other file in Google Drive.
  • Basic profile (email, name): to show which account is signed in.

The sign-in token stays in the browser tab and is removed when you sign out or close the tab. The dashboard does not send your data to us or to anyone else.

Meta data (Instagram, Facebook, Threads)

ARIVU publishes approved posts to our own Instagram professional account, Facebook Page and Threads profile, and reads their insights (views, likes, followers).

How data is stored

  • Content and statistics are stored in our own Google Sheets and Google Drive.
  • Access tokens are stored as encrypted secrets (GitHub Actions Secrets) or on our own computer. They are never published.
  • We do not sell, rent or share any data with third parties, and we do not use it for advertising.

Removing access

We can remove ARIVU's access at any time from Google Account permissions and from the Meta "Business Integrations" settings. Preview files can be deleted from Google Drive. Anyone who used the ARIVU Dashboard can remove its access the same way.
